Warehouse Picker / Packer — Pune

We run a fulfilment centre in Chakan, Pune and are hiring pickers and packers for our day and
night shifts.

The work:
- Pick items from shelf locations using a handheld scanner
- Pack orders into cartons and apply shipping labels
- Move totes and cartons around the floor
- Report damaged stock to the shift supervisor
- Meet an hourly pick target set by the supervisor

Requirements:
- Able to lift up to 15 kg repeatedly through a shift
- Able to stand and walk for the full shift
- Basic numeracy for counting and verifying quantities
- Comfortable using a handheld barcode scanner after training
- Prior warehouse experience preferred but we do train freshers

Shifts:
Day shift 8 AM to 5 PM, night shift 8 PM to 5 AM. Six days a week. Night shift carries an
additional allowance of Rs 3,000 per month.

Pay:
Rs 16,000 to Rs 21,000 per month based on shift and experience. Overtime paid at 1.5x the
hourly rate. Monthly salary credited by the 7th.

Provided:
- Safety shoes and gloves
- Subsidised canteen meal each shift
- Free shuttle from Chakan and Bhosari pickup points

Location: Chakan, Pune
Employment type: Full time, on-roll
