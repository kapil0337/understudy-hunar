Delivery Rider — Chennai

We are hiring delivery riders for our Chennai operations across Adyar, Velachery and T. Nagar.

About the role:
You will pick up orders from partner restaurants and deliver them to customers within your
assigned zone. Most deliveries are within 5 km. You will use our rider app to accept and
complete orders.

What we need:
- Own two-wheeler in working condition
- Valid two-wheeler driving licence
- Android smartphone
- Comfortable riding in Chennai city traffic
- Able to read basic English or Tamil addresses on the app

Shift:
Two shifts available — morning (7 AM to 3 PM) and evening (3 PM to 11 PM). Six days a week,
one weekly off. You choose your shift when you join.

Pay:
Rs 18,000 to Rs 24,000 per month depending on shift and number of deliveries completed.
Fuel allowance of Rs 2,500 per month paid separately. Weekly payouts every Monday.

Also provided:
- Company t-shirt and delivery bag
- Accident insurance cover
- Rs 1,000 joining bonus after 50 completed deliveries

Experience: Freshers welcome. Prior delivery experience is a plus but not required.

Location: Chennai (Adyar, Velachery, T. Nagar zones)
Employment type: Full time, on-roll
