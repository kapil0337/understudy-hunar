Retail Sales Associate — Bengaluru

Our apparel brand is expanding and we need retail sales associates for our Indiranagar and
Koramangala stores in Bengaluru.

Responsibilities:
- Greet walk-in customers and help them find what they are looking for
- Explain product features, sizes and current offers
- Handle billing at the point-of-sale counter
- Keep the shop floor tidy and restock shelves
- Support stock counts at month end

Requirements:
- Minimum 1 year of experience in retail, hospitality or customer-facing sales
- Comfortable speaking Kannada and English with customers; Hindi is a plus
- Able to stand for the length of a shift
- Basic comfort with a billing system or willingness to learn

Shift:
Rotational shifts between 10 AM and 10 PM, five days a week with two rotating weekly offs.
Weekend availability is required as those are our busiest days.

Compensation:
Rs 20,000 to Rs 26,000 per month, plus a monthly store-level sales incentive of up to Rs 4,000.
Provident fund and ESI as per statute.

Benefits:
- Staff discount of 30 percent on all merchandise
- Uniform provided
- Annual performance review

Location: Bengaluru (Indiranagar and Koramangala stores)
Employment type: Full time, permanent
